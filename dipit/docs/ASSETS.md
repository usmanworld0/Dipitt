# Current DIPITT 3D asset sources

Updated 16 November 2026: real 3D packaging now uses DIPITT artwork from the official front and back photos. The label renderer converts photographed cylindrical labels into angular UV maps, then wraps those maps around cylindrical meshes. Bottle bodies, shoulders, glass shells, cap ribs and Sriracha nozzles are separate geometry. Pouches use beveled extruded bodies, front/back artwork and a separate spout. Shapes are presentation reconstructions from the product photographs.

All 27 packs have matching transparent WebP model renders in `public/assets/models/`, generated by `scripts/render-assets.mjs` using the same components as the live carousel and product viewer. These renders are used for shop tiles, bag items, shared transitions and initial/fallback posters. Browser caches do not replace the original photos.

Original DIPITT front/back photos and wordmarks remain under `public/assets/dipitt/`. Image provenance is recorded in `dipitt-image-sources.json`, sourced from https://www.dipitt.com/collections/sauces. Original editorial food photography and font information are retained below with the historical concept asset log.

# Original DIPT assets

All runtime assets are local. No Agrumea images, brand artwork, logos or licensed fonts are loaded by the application.

## 3D packaging

`components/3d/Bottle.tsx`: original lathed bottle, tapered shoulder and neck, sauce surface, transparent reflective shell, glass base, ribbed cap and tamper ring. Cap ribs use GPU instancing. `textures.ts` creates deterministic 2048×1024 cylindrical labels with fronts, side ingredient copy, back typography and a decorative barcode. The barcode is not a real product identifier. Five colourways represent the five conceptual DIPT sauces.

`public/assets/bottle-*.webp` are transparent renders of those actual models, exported by `node scripts/render-assets.mjs`. The live homepage and product pages use the same geometry, textures and studio lighting. Collection, gallery fallbacks, shopping bag and selected-bottle transition use the lightweight local renders. Bottle renders total approximately 145KB.

## Typography

Bodoni Moda (display) and DM Sans (body) downloaded from Google Fonts, served locally as `bodoni.ttf` and `dm-sans.ttf`. Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. They substitute for the reference’s licensed IvyMode and IvyStyle Sans. Source URLs are recorded in `docs/font-source.css`.

## Brand graphics

Chilli logo, favicon, loading peppers, geometric background and circular typography are original code-native SVG/CSS artwork. Illustration and packaging details are for the DIPT concept. Recipes, heat levels, sizes and prices are illustrative, not verified commercial product specifications.
